Who We Are

Ohana Means Family

In Hawaiian culture, ohana refers to family in a broad sense — including blood relatives, adoptive kin, and intentional family. No one gets left behind.

🏠

Community Resilience

Supporting disaster recovery and building sustainable infrastructure that helps communities thrive in challenging times.

🎁

Gift Economy

Practicing the art of giving without expectation, building trust and abundance through generosity and reciprocity.

🌿

Cultural Preservation

Honoring and protecting Hawaiian traditions, sacred lands, and indigenous knowledge for future generations.

Our Mission

Strengthening Maui Through Connection

After the 2023 Lahaina fires, our community came together like never before. OHANA Association was born from this spirit of mutual aid and collective healing.

We believe that true resilience comes not from individual strength, but from the bonds we form with one another. Through volunteer work, skill-sharing, and resource pooling, we're rebuilding not just infrastructure — but hope.
